WHO WE ARE

We are Brown Step Bank from Rwanda. We help our customers understand the good thing of saving money, saving rates, bonds and loans. We teach people in simple way so everybody can grow.

1. Saving Rates

Saving rates means the extra money we add when you keep your money with us.

Example: If you save 100,000 RWF, we add 8% after one year. So you get 108,000 RWF.

Good thing: Your money grows even when you sleep. Good for school fees and business.

2. Bonds

Bonds means you lend money to the bank or government for some years, and we pay you back with extra.

Example: 1 year bond = 9%, 3 years bond = 11% in Rwanda.

Good thing: Bonds give more money than normal saving. Safe and strong for future.

3. Other Things We Do

Loans: We give loans to farmers, shops and students to start business.

Mobile Banking: You can send and save money with your phone with Brown Step App.

Advice: Come to our branch in Kigali, Huye, Musanze and we explain everything for free.

How We Help You

1. We explain saving in Kinyarwanda, English and French.
2. We show you how to open savings account in 10 minutes.
3. We help you choose: normal saving for every day, or bonds for long time.
