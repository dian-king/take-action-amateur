WHAT WE DO

At Brown Step Bank we keep your money safe and help it grow. Here are the 3 main things we do every day in Rwanda.

1. Save Money

We keep your money safe and add extra every year.

Normal saving = 6% per year. Student saving = 7%. Business saving = 8%.

Example: Save 50,000 RWF per month in Kigali branch, after 1 year you have 600,000 + bonus.

2. Give Loans

We give loans to help you start or grow.

School loan: pay school fees and pay back small small.
Business loan: up to 5,000,000 RWF for shops in Huye, Musanze, Kigali.
Farm loan: for seeds and cows, pay back after harvest.

3. Mobile Banking

You dont need to come to bank every time. Use phone.

*182# or Brown Step App: send money, check balance, save money.

Good thing: fast, day and night, even on Sunday.
